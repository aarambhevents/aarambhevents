Good to know

Is it family-friendly?

Yes. There's a whole kids' party, and babysitters are onsite for kids aged 2 to 7. Guests drinking alcohol must be 21+ with valid ID.

Is it secure?

Yes. Onsite officers and COP are present, and both parties run under one secured roof.

Do tickets include a table?

General Admission doesn't include a reserved table, but there's ample seating in the dining halls, some seating for the shows and a limited number of highboy tables. For your own table, book a VIP Cabana Table.

Parking and hotels?

Parking is free during the event (cars left after 10 AM the next day may be towed). Discounted hotel rates are available to attendees.

Can I get a refund?

Tickets are non-refundable. Early-bird prices run through Nov 30, and prices are dynamic after that, so booking early gets the best price.

How do I book a VIP cabana?

Pay by Zelle, or call or WhatsApp 678-457-7761 to book.
